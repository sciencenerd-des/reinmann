# Fixed logarithmic forecasts — 17 October 2026

This is a finite-family predictive test, not an exchange-rate theorem. The protocol and forecasts were recorded before either new mixture parameter, atlas or window trial. The registration is local and hash-bound; no independent public time stamp is claimed.

## Plain control and decay measurement

The primary control is the real primitive quadratic character modulo 13, Conrey label 12. It is an actual single Euler-product L-function. Its even gamma factor has alpha=1/4; the mixture controls have odd gamma alpha=3/4. Thus transferring a decay rate by conductor alone is an explicit hypothesis, not an established normalization invariance.

The ordinary log-derivative coefficients are chi(p^k)*log(p), with zero coefficients away from prime powers. The Fourier correlation already includes both translations, so multiplying these coefficients by two would be a normalization error. An initial failed probe made that error; its diagnostic is retained and excluded from accepted results. Independent tests compare the complex-character coefficients with the Dirichlet recurrence and the even-gamma single-L entries with the original integral representation.

A secondary complex-character probe uses Conrey label2 and the real parity restriction, equivalently the conjugation-symmetrized form. It is not the primary single-L decay measurement and is not substituted for the quadratic control after looking at target data.

The primary measurement uses finite Fourier blocks at C=16,20,24,28,32 and N128, with outward eigenvalue enclosures at 768bits. The ground eigenvalue is in the even sector. Its log-linear slope is

    kappa13 = 0.9155751479199306.

The N96 fit gives0.913981855265866. Adjacent N128 slopes range from0.88864 to0.96658; the log-fit RMSE is0.06663. The first three eigenvalues of each parity and their separate slopes are retained. Supplemental N192 spectra test resolution without changing the frozen forecast. These are finite eigenvalues and descriptive slopes, not full-operator eigenvalue bounds or an asymptotic constant.

Platt's [Numerical Computations Concerning the GRH, v1, Theorem7.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1305.3087) verifies primitive conductor13 L-functions to height10^8/13. This provides external finite-height context; it is not a proof of global GRH and is not used to infer finite-matrix positivity or to close an infinite zero-sum tail.
