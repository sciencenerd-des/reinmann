## Reading limitations that matter

Recent preprints are leads, not automatically accepted results. Two reviewed revisions explicitly withdraw broader earlier claims. Independent reproduction and examination of all analytic bounds are prerequisites for making an external computational result part of a proof. The report's strategic recommendation does not depend on treating every 2026 claim as established.

The next deeper reading should concentrate on the exact operator domains in papers 1–3, the full certificate and comparison-operator argument in paper 5, and the full text of paper 17. Reading additional distant RH proposals has lower immediate value than resolving those precise compatibility questions.
